## Closure and future retirement

A MEM01 function, definition, skeleton, header or guard binding has exactly
three payloads: declaration owner, subject and source-guard owner.
A MEM02 mapping binding likewise has three.
A MEM02 fixed-input binding has four, adding its generator owner.

These exact scopes exclude collateral shared parsers and observation
policy. The unchanged EV01 closure builder still adds the fact owner and
every payload. A deliberate overlap with a real subject, declaration or
assertion owner must continue to fail `PROJECTION`.

This prerequisite installs no new assertion selector. Every current guard
continues to reject its original negative controls.

A future correction must:

- declare the exact invalidated new keys and any other still-active
  historical identities;
- explicitly refine the corresponding current source/behavior contract;
- keep the new declaration's historical values unchanged;
- retain every unrelated clause and permanent invariant;
- bind any necessary current-source, helper-closure and primitive-observation
  adaptation independently before its actual Red;
- compile and execute actual current bodies rather than projecting a changed
  algorithm back into historical code;
- preserve exact historical native modes and original work/semantic oracles;
- complete actual Red, Green and affected-unit recertification; and
- wire only authorized assertion consumers after that transition permits it.

In particular, MEM03 must resolve its complete old/new native observation
and helper closure after this repair. Granular identity coverage alone
does not certify those future adaptations or its selected-record algorithm.

## Failure behavior

Reject:

- missing, extra or duplicate declaration assignments or rows;
- nonliteral values or executable declaration statements;
- an unknown task, scope kind, subject, assertion owner or row shape;
- an omitted original function, helper, fixed native member or comparison
  dimension;
- duplicate ownership of a fixed input's two consumers;
- a function or subject borrowed from another scope;
- changed reference commit/tree, original value or expected comparison
  normalization;
- a malformed or linked declaration, subject or assertion-owner path;
- incomplete predecessor-record/fingerprint conservation;
- a new identity with no real existing assertion; and
- any actual immutable/candidate overlap.

Failure is explicit, never a fallback to an aggregate, inferred owner,
current filesystem glob or newly computed candidate expectation.
